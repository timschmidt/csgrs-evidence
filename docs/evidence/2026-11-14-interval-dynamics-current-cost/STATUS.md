## Complete instruction attribution

All 582 Callgrind observations complete: every one of the 194 workloads in each
variant, 32 warmed completed-result/destruction callbacks. Four instrumentation
workers are used only after wall timing finishes. Their elapsed times are not
performance measurements. Raw profiles are losslessly compressed with decoded
hashes; per-function self counts sum to each profile's instruction total.

Relative to baseline, extraction has 101 increased, 15 decreased and 78 unchanged
instruction totals; provider control has 95 increased, 21 decreased and 78
unchanged. These are single instrumented observations, not deterministic bounds.
All six longer-timed cases have identical total AND per-function self counts in
all variants. Consequently instruction-count reductions alone do not explain or
clear their reproduced runtime failures. No unique hardware cause is established.

Tangent alignment changes 240,808 to 241,704 instructions per epoch (+28/call),
with attribution moving between the exact-rational product-sum layers. Corner
lookahead reproduces 2,503,560 baseline, 2,508,360 extraction and 2,507,720 provider
instructions. Attribution, including report-drop renames, remains explicit;
demangled names can combine generic instantiations. Fifty-seven bounded function
disassemblies from the real owner artifacts are retained with exact commands.
Their sizes/addresses are observations; the observer's code generation is not
assumed identical to the timing executable. Layout/cache behavior remains a
hypothesis, not a demonstrated explanation or permission to relax acceptance.
