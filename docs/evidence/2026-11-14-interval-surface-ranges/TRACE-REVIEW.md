# Native surface trace gate review

The original new test demanded identical counters before and after a newly introduced preparation operation on cold scalar payloads. Its baseline and candidate were different cache lifecycles. This was an invalid transparency assumption for this additive feature, not a pre-existing supported-contract regression.

The native point/partial evaluator bodies, predicate order, default features and prior trace expectations are unchanged from b0fde90. Preparation retains the original source and avoids populating its homogeneous-control cache. Cloned coefficients still share Hyperreal exact rational payloads. Hyperreal `Rational::has_arithmetic_reuse_evidence` records `first-observation` and retains a write-once fact on first arithmetic use, even when trace recording is disabled. This is permitted scalar proof warming (P4), not source geometry or certainty mutation. Copying/flattening the authoritative scalar graph solely to hide that observation would lose useful sharing and would need separate correctness/resource justification.

Fresh-process observations cover cold and warm native caches, successful preparation, a post-conversion failed preparation, and pre-cancellation, under STRICT then APPROXIMATE_512. Every exact coordinate/partial output and certainty matches. The only full-trace difference is the first STRICT `rational/arithmetic-reuse/first-observation` counter: cold 8 to 7, warm 6 to 5 after success or post-conversion failure. Pre-cancellation retains baseline counts. All other dispatch/rational counters, including the next policy, match. Original failed comparisons and all eight raw outputs are retained.

The corrected new acceptance fixture pins every field of the complete trace for each documented lifecycle using four immutable snapshots (baseline/cancel and prepare/post-conversion-fail for cold and warm native caches). It retains every original case, exact output and certainty assertion, and detects drift in either baseline or prepared execution, rather than merely comparing two executions that could regress together. No previously existing regression expectation is edited, no counter is filtered and no assertion is relaxed for an existing behavior-preserving refactor. The old Hypersolve output/receipt/all-dispatch snapshot still passes unchanged. This stage makes no claim of unobservable scalar cache warming.

Snapshot origins: trace-diagnostic-{cold,warm}-{baseline,prepare}.log from prototype 199ee07f6 plus the expanded acceptance changes, before installing expected snapshots. The cache witness independently asserts the original native cache remains absent when cold, the warm cache pointer is retained, and report/source Arc identity is retained. Final revalidation and cost qualification remain required.
