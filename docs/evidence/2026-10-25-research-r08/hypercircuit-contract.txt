P8, P9, P14, P18, P19, P25 and P30. Existing classify_point/inset/contains_disc/

BoardBoundaryGeometryError>> for each complete query under its explicit policy.

queries and symbolic z = sin(e+1)-sin(e)cos(1)-cos(e)sin(1) = 0 coordinates or
clearance. The independent trigonometric identity establishes the expected

consumption, strict/approximate/strict, source drop, mapping, and error paths
must retain their actual receipt. Do not infer a certified successful result
from the receipt of an uncertain or failed operation.

receipt directly; old methods remain documented compatibility interfaces.
