# Repeated five-repair consumer resources: finite measurement protocol

This continues the frozen resource-controls record at evidence commit
b2dde626edd2ca1a4b4e5030192c3108d345b32a. It changes no production source,
fixture, monitor, cap or accepted account. The owning layer, properties,
consumer/domain, source boundaries and prospective numerical caps are those in
the frozen resource-controls PROTOCOL.md. Keep all original standalone,
four-repair and five-repair control failures and positive charges.

Use the sealed resource images from the control stage, with full SHA-256 checks
before and after measurement. Ordinary sources: fixed/current/alpha/linalg/
Jacobian/domain/four/five; cases 0..3; STRICT and APPROXIMATE_512; reused or
rebuilt inputs; no trace, compiled-passive trace, active trace; cold/warm;
three fresh process repetitions. Each combination measures operation/cleanup
allocations, operation stack with patterns 165 and 90, and cleanup stack with
both patterns. Added cases 4..6 use only final source and added-feature images.
This is 12,600 processes with six independent process workers; each measured
consumer has one worker. It is not a six-worker shared-cache workload claim.

Warm allocation runs eight prior complete operations. Warm stack uses the
unchanged monitor's one prior callback, then measures replacement of its output.
Cleanup stack starts from one completed output and destroys it and the reused
input, when present. These prehistories remain separate. Compare full output
and trace strings across matched source/feature/mode/pattern/repetition histories;
hashes are integrity checks, not substitutes for equality. Preserve every raw
log, including failures. Original cold-reuse resource controls, including the
first collector attempt's successful fixed/default control, are supplementary
observations in matching cost maxima; do not discard them or earlier charges.

The process limit is 60 seconds each, total driver deadline 7,200 seconds.
Use an external 8 MiB regular-file output limit per process, kill the process
group on timeout, and record external containment separately from typed solver
exhaustion. At most six jobs are in flight. Lossless compressed logs stop new
submissions at 512 MiB; at most six 8 MiB outputs may finish after that threshold.
Plan/JSON metadata is capped at 64 MiB, giving a 640 MiB total stage ceiling.
Transient raw outputs use RAM, at most 48 MiB. Preserve development-volume
20 GiB headroom and require 1 GiB staging free space before launch. No infinite
retry, sample replacement, discarded failure or silent deadline reset.

Incremental and cumulative positive charges retain the fixed c9f1f9ac baseline.
Keep all matched modes/configurations separate. Requested and allocator-usable
payloads include paired operation, report cleanup and input cleanup epochs.
Signed post-drop deltas are observations, not absolute persistent footprints;
never divide by negative/censored baselines. The independent whole-owned
32-cycle control histories remain separate and their ten failed metrics stay.
Reused-input whole-owned history qualification remains open.

Touched stack uses the unchanged x86_64 C callback monitor, byte scan resolution,
two fill patterns, full callee red zone, guarded 2 MiB mapping and recorded
caller/guard/untouched bytes. It excludes frames above the callback boundary;
zero or guard-reaching measurements cannot establish an uncensored bound.
Account for the full 2 MiB mapping in the worker payload capacity, plus the
maximum paired heap footprint (without summing retained and peak maxima).
This is a qualification payload allowance, not allocator-arena/RSS, firmware
RAM or universal input/depth proof. Native image headroom remains separate.

Hold only the verified original quiet watcher (PID/start checked), while the
workflow parent remains stopped. Restore the same watcher in finally without
resetting its fixed six-hour horizon. Unrelated jobs and frozen queued commands
are untouched. External concurrent work means elapsed time is not evidence of
quiet performance. Preserve terminal progress on any failure and restore the
watcher. Source admission, runtime, package/WASM, cancellation/resource contract
and full release validation remain open regardless of these measurements.
