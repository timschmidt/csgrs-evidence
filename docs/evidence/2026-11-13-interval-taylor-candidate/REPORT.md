one shared flat graph and memoized node/variable derivatives. Original outputs
remain first, preserving guards even under zero derivatives. Existing derivative
rules supply the local authority. A shared macro preserves the old loop's call
and temporary order; the first forced-inline closure helper was rejected because
its emitted function union grew by 3,066 bytes.

TaylorModel retains exact Real coefficients, exact singleton dyadic centers, a
finite original box, full normalized next-order derivative bounds, every signed
remainder contribution, original source, and regularity evidence. Multivariate
integral Taylor remainder on the convex box gives f(x) in P(x)+R. An exhaustive
primitive-grammar review binds analyticity to the current open-domain regularity
checks; arbitrary C1 is not assumed analytic. The final image intersects this
bound with an independently certified natural source enclosure. Subdomain reuse
keeps the original global remainder and grid. No model arithmetic/composition,
wrapping-control or ODE-existence claim is introduced.

Hyperdynamics supplies an optional midpoint-centered field consumer, preserving
exact time/state interpretation, original requests and native empty/unit products.
It uses the shared graph/native arithmetic budget and exposes the complete owner
evidence. Midpoint representation failures, invalid extrapolation, stronger
regularity failures and resource exhaustion remain typed. Existing scalar
refinement/payload, heap-byte and within-scalar cancellation exclusions remain;
these are not whole-work bounds. Failed construction publishes no model. Replay
reconstructs source and requests, never trusts proof flags.

Independent witnesses cover polynomial/binomial and high-factorial coefficients,
mixed variables, exact nonrational sine/cosine coefficients, algebraic identities,
nonzero rational-function and sine tails, source guards, empty/unit products,
subdomain use and further field consumption, cancellation, precise final budgets,

[0,1/4], with coefficients [1/4,0,-1] and zero remainder, versus the unchanged
natural enclosure [0,1]. The original empty-subdomain bug and the incorrectly
