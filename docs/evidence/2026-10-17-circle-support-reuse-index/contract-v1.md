those of E45: ordered pairs of supported circular curves, exact rational keys
where eligible, original structural equality for symbolic and very-large
coefficients, original retained witnesses, one policy per owned batch.

+10.5638344% median, all seven paired differences positive and separate ranges.

the current production-equivalent revision before drafting a successor.

ordered equality search and avoid key construction on misses. Once activated,
keep the existing exact-key lookup, earlier-unkeyed-match priority and insertion
