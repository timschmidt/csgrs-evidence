# Domains beyond conical LINE exchange

Keep the admitted LINE-revolution and native continuation corpus as the next
baseline. This extension does not make arbitrary revolutions interchangeable
with native cones, or establish general curved-solid Boolean closure.

- Native contact and face-arrangement work remains the route to broader CAD
  Booleans (WP03/WP06/WP07/WP08). Retain selected contacts, complete edge images,
  orientation and source witnesses through stitching; do not enlarge the solid
  recognizer by dropping coverage requirements. Fillets, shelling and broader
  sweeps still depend on their own complete intersection/embedding proofs.
- Exact B-spline-to-line recognition belongs in the native curve layers. A
  collinear control polygon or endpoint fit alone does not supply the required
  whole-curve image and parameter correspondence. A finite generator also has
  a source domain, which a replacement infinite LINE must not erase. Preserve
  monotonic branches, endpoint/root identity, traversal, singular parameters
  and the domain consumed by every face/pcurve. The original
  `cone-finite-bspline.step` remains the independent unsupported fixture until
  that native proof and the complete edited STEP workflow pass.
- Apex-ending solids require native singular-chart/topology admission before
  the adapter may publish them. A valid cone support and a non-apex branch
  witness do not prove an apex vertex, degenerate boundary, cap or closed solid.
  Add native constructor, classification, measure, transform, Boolean and cold
  replay acceptance before mapping the corresponding STEP boundary entities.
- Direct `CONICAL_SURFACE` is a distinct source chart and encoding obligation.
  Qualify its reference placement/radius, parameter correspondence and exact
  semi-angle representability separately. A finite decimal angle cannot stand
  in for an exact native atan value. Preserve the LINE alternative where it
  exactly describes the same native carrier.
- Extend source accounting and durable operation provenance without treating
  native JSON as persistence of the external STEP document. Current per-face
  maps are import-scoped mathematical correspondences; references to authored
  entity records require the original document.
- Retained-memory, total-work and cancellation admission, clean documented
  packages, actual bindings, broader independent exporters, directed fuzz/ASan/
  lexicase, coverage and release qualification remain separate gates. Existing
  parser/encoding counts and source-path WASM tests do not close them.

Supporting crate changes are appropriate when these BREP features require them.
Place each invariant in its owning crate, reproduce its blocker on the current
upstream-integrated revision, preserve the admitted consumer corpus, and record
new successful-work costs separately from earlier failures. Unrelated upstream
repairs remain with the concurrent thread.
