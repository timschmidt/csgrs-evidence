# Adverse cost review and retention decision

The existing `unequal_holes` group remains an expected typed rejection with the
original input, assertions, iteration count and unchanged source model. Across
three alternating matched pairs, median complete-group time rises from
2.696819 ms to 3.301229 ms (ratio 1.224120). Allocation calls rise from 9,123 to
11,483 (25.87%); cumulative requested bytes rise from 1,188,472 to 1,422,805
(19.72%). These measurements include construction, validation, the rejected
partial-height subtraction and archive checks. They are not a faster successful
operation, and the increase is not dismissed as noise.

The source path now has an additional exact planar-intersection attempt after
the unequal-profile test and failed graph path. It can prove the disjoint
products needed by the original XOR continuation; overlapping unequal-hole
profiles still retain their previous unsupported result. The isolated cost of
that individual query has not been measured, so the full-group increase is not
attributed quantitatively to one helper.

Two existing complete spline groups also show small allocation increases; every
row, including all slower observations below the review marker, remains in
[costs](costs.md) and the [resource record](resource-review.json). No fixture,
policy, proof, materialization or provenance was removed. The original successful
artifacts and semantic consumers all retain their contracts.

Retain this increment for the new exact completed workflows, with the rejection
cost explicitly open. It is a completeness extension, not an optimization or a
claim that performance has not regressed. Do not use later favorable ratios to
cancel this observation or earlier adverse campaigns.

For a separate optimization, first establish a frozen complete-work baseline.
An available exact common-interior witness could reject the disjoint-product
rule before constructing the full intersection, but bounds or equal outer
contours cannot prove that fact. Such a witness/query belongs in Hypercurve,
must preserve whole-input semantics and typed uncertainty, and has not been
implemented or measured here. Preserve the full historical 192-case catalogue,
later complete consumer cohorts, current deterministic artifacts, shared-face
and tiny-overlap controls, and the unequal-hole rejection when evaluating it.
