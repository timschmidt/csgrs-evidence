# Plane sections and rational images

Hypersolve owns homogeneous bivariate composition and the active total degree used for rational substitution. This avoids adding unnecessary denominator powers on every repeated image. It does not impose a total-work or coefficient-size bound.

Hypercurve owns `RationalMap2`, ordered maps on retained polynomial points and branches, original pole guards, conservative image bounds and the sufficient whole-rectangle injectivity certificate. Points retain one joint selected fiber; independent reconstructed coordinates never replace that authority. A normalized homogeneous Jacobian numerator and strict interval principal-minor tests prove positive definiteness of the symmetric derivative. The segment argument on the convex rectangle then proves injectivity. An inconclusive certificate is a typed blocker, not evidence that the map is noninjective. `research.md` records the primary reference and the implementation inference.

For whole-image parameterization equality, identical definitions take the cheap path. Otherwise, retained source/slab/root identity must agree; the owner pulls both coordinate fractions back through their complete map chains and proves their cross-product residuals divisible by the defining equation. This is a sufficient whole-branch proof, including at singular events. Restrictions and traversal flags do not alter the evaluation chart; callers check the actual domains independently. Original denominator guards were checked during image construction and are never cancelled by this comparison.

HyperBRep owns the exact homogeneous tensor/plane pullback, complete UV decomposition, spatial lifting, both pcurves and the separate spatial-image certificate. A complete UV graph alone is insufficient: the map into the plane must be proved injective over the whole box before a nonempty polynomial section is published. Strict one-sided control nets and coplanar nets have direct proofs. Existing admitted linear-axis and bilinear paths remain fast paths.

`SurfaceSurfaceIntersection` contact points now retain `GeometricPoint3`, including nonrational isolated contacts. Explicit represented-coordinate consumers use `try_coordinates`; unsupported consumers retain typed failures. `restrict_source` retains selected endpoints in the original chart. Model image validation checks the pullback identity and both rational image coordinate identities; no sampled endpoint check substitutes for whole-image agreement.

A strengthened downstream test exposed the need for the coordinate-residual proof. After affine transport, orthogonal projection into the transformed frame can differ from the original map by a multiple of the section equation. Structural map equality incorrectly refused a mathematically identical image. The exact divisibility proof fixes this in Hypercurve. Tests re-run both pcurve image proofs after cold replay and after affine transport plus replay. A negative surface has identical endpoint images but a different interior and is rejected.

Archive 14 requires ordered rational maps on each polynomial branch and permits rational images of retained joint points. Replay reconstructs complete source graphs, checks selectors and ranges, and validates every original map guard before publishing images. Metadata admission counts map records, rows, coefficients and stored degree before scalar decoding. It is not an expansion, heap, stack or cancellation guarantee. The deliberate 13-to-14 migration changes only the top-level version and adds an empty maps array to old branch records; there is no compatibility decoder.

The new carrier models contain geometry, edges and both pcurves; they are not closed solids or trimmed faces. Tests explicitly validate the two surface images before and after replay. General polynomial trim arrangements, derivative carriers, noninjective spatial images, general NURBS sections, arbitrary surface/surface intersections and their solid Booleans remain open.
