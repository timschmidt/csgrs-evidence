# Exact expectations

The full original quadratic parallel-cap fixture is retained, including its three
quadratic straight closures. Its source quadratic at t=1/2 is (2,1); the unit
left parallel is (2,2). After the radial translation by three, the revolved
boundary therefore contains (5,0,2) and (0,5,2). At z=0, radius five is interior;
z=3 is exterior. The revolution axis remains exterior.

The original Boolean-clipped cap has x in [0,1] and lower boundary y=-2. After
radial translation, its solid at z=0 has radii in [3,4]. Radius 7/2 is inside,
3 and 4 are boundary, and 5 is outside. Exact displacements of 1/10^12 on either
side of these bounds stay distinct. The z=-2 boundary and a point 1/10^12 below
it remain distinct. Samples are also checked on the negative angular quadrant,
and transported independently through the tested similarities and reflections.

A rectangular cavity with radii [13/4,15/4] and heights [-1/2,1/2] excludes radius
7/2 at z=0, while radius 25/8 remains material and 13/4 is cavity boundary. A
scaled selected cap supplies an independently selected cavity in the complete
original nonlinear profile. Touching, crossing and nested cavities are rejected.

The native annular control with radii two and four and height two has exact volume
24*pi. Its unified-region construction must retain native Line/CircleArc geometry.
The disconnected-region fixture translates the selected region and its hole by
five in axial height; exactly one solid owns each material sample and neither
owns either cavity sample. Empty curve differences stay empty after revolution
and participate in the supported empty-body Boolean identities.

These exact geometric expectations establish more than agreement between two
wrappers or stable checksums. Cold replay, deterministic bytes, shared-incidence
subdivision, typed STEP rejection, certainty and resource recovery are checked
separately. Measurements for procedural profiles remain explicitly unavailable
where an exact integral is unsupported.
