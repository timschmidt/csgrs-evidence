# Procedural solid revolution admission contract

The production owner is HyperBRep construction and topology admission. Hypercurve
owns filled planar regions, source charts, contacts, winding and selected roots;
Hyperreal/Hyperlimit own exact expressions and predicate evidence. HyperBRep IO
owns STEP encoding. This change adds no alternate numerical kernel, dependency,
compatibility adapter, persistence schema, or public approximation operation.

`builder::revolve_path` and `builder::revolve_path_region` now accept the retained
procedural profile domain. `builder::revolve_curve_region` consumes Hypercurve's
filled-region carrier, including holes, disconnected material and canonical
empty results, and returns the existing `BodySet`. Native line/circle images
continue through the existing construction path. Production edits remain in
HyperBRep; HyperBRep IO gains a public negative exchange-boundary regression.

The added domain is a simple radial/axial path with a certified strictly positive
radial enclosure, representable exact scalar charts, and a common meridian plane
whose projection is a certified similarity. A complete turn forms the boundary
of the profile region times the circle. Strictly contained, pairwise-disjoint
cavity paths use inward shells. The certificate is replayed from ordinary faces,
curves, pcurves, parameter correspondences and incidence; there is no privileged
constructor flag. The exact source curve, selected endpoint fields and root
identity remain authoritative. Rendering samples and optional metrics do not
supply topology evidence. Conservative bounds can still reject a truly positive
profile when positivity is not established by that enclosure.

The result continues through supported similarities/reflections, edge splits,
strict point classification, native export/cold import/validation, cavities,
multiple components and empty identities. Exact offsets of 1/10^12 stay distinct.
No claim is made that general curved solid Booleans or arbitrary affine transport
are supported. Unavailable procedural area/volume remains a measurement error;
STEP currently reports its existing face-attributed unsupported revolution
surface. Neither error is converted into approximate geometry or an empty solid.

Work/precision scopes and archive limits retain their existing contracts. A
zero-work cold import exhausts and a subsequent ordinary import succeeds.
Strict/approximate-policy/strict calls on the exact fixture remain certified;
selecting an approximate policy alone does not consume its terminal. This is not
an approximate-construction provenance test. No cache policy was changed.
Whole-operation total work, retained heap, coefficient growth, cancellation and
global cold/evicted/disabled cache controls remain open. Commands use external
timeouts; timing uses reconstructed inputs and records first and repeated calls.

The seven outcome categories are kept separate. Successful strict workflows
produce certified results, including valid empty results. Invalid topology and
axis/cavity violations fail. Procedural STEP and unavailable metrics remain
unsupported. Instrumented work exhaustion is exercised. No new undecided or
qualified approximate successful workflow is claimed, and no incorrect result
was observed in the qualified corpus. Group counts include positive and negative
assertions and are not counts of independent geometric capabilities.

This is source-local feature qualification, not release or full pre-push
qualification. Installed distributions, package feature/MSRV coherence, affected
production bindings, broad fuzz/ASan/lexicase campaigns, formal program proof and
measured coverage remain open. All 28 development work packages remain open or
in progress. The consumed one-time eaa0cae54 push authorization does not apply to
this increment.
