# Follow-up: cost of exact angular certificates

This is a proposed experiment, not an implemented optimization. The retained
[complete campaign](benchmark-summary.json) and
[scalar/predicate supplement](dependency-costs-v1/summary.json) are the starting
evidence. Keep every raw sample and all prior adverse campaigns. Shared-host
ratios do not isolate a cause or establish a statistical performance claim.

The changed scalar owner is
[`computable/node/algebra.rs`](../../../../hyperreal/src/computable/node/algebra.rs).
The BREP feature needs the principal-angle certificate after repeated native
subdivision and archive replay. Reverting that capability or accepting a new
`Unknown` is not a cost repair. Properties P2–P5, P8–P11, P18–P20 and P27/P36
apply; supporting changes belong in their sufficient owning crate.

Static inspection suggests three places to measure before choosing a change:

1. The atan projection now computes an exact rational square root to decide
   whether a tangent admits the shared acos basis. Measure repeated success
   and failure separately, including irrational roots and signed quadratics.
2. Complementary acos basis reconciliation proves both signs and the exact
   unit-circle equation. Check whether consumers repeatedly reconstruct that
   same immutable certificate.
3. A failed direct linear projection can invoke the existing Laurent authority.
   `exact_sign` retries inverse-angle proofs before its cached-Unknown return,
   because stronger evidence can become available. Measure this path in the
   retained inverse-witness, circle-import and pcurve workflows before adding
   another cache or restricting the projection's domain.

The completed 134-case scalar subset supplies four adverse controls:
`atan2_quadrant_three_negative_pi` (1.393584),
`atan2_quadrant_two_pi_correction` (1.452677), `atan_tan_6pi_7` (1.142970),
and `real_cotangent/atan_two_quotient_construct` (1.165437). These are observed
case-median ratios, not isolated attribution to a helper. Use the unchanged
primitive bodies as controls alongside the completed BREP workflows.

Prefer sharing an existing positive certificate or avoiding demonstrably
redundant projection. A structural negative certificate must distinguish
unsupported shape from budget exhaustion or unavailable evidence; the latter
cannot be permanently cached as false. Any cache needs explicit identity,
lifetime, precision/policy admissibility, serialization reset, retained-memory
accounting and cold/warm/concurrent-state tests. A cheap guard may defer an
expensive proof only when another path still preserves the complete accepted
domain. Do not add a BREP-specific scalar expression recognizer.

Freeze the retained feature binaries before the experiment. Profile the full
existing fixtures, then run a small comparison with unchanged exact receipts,
policies, iterations, output materialization and cache conditions. If that
candidate is justified, rerun the complete native/IO and affected dependency
checks, all 170 BREP/STEP/curve cases, the dependency microcost supplement,
repeated subdivision/reload growth probe, preserved continuation frontier and
54 deterministic old artifacts. The three new torus workflows are successful
baseline cases for that future comparison. Include direct unresolved aggregate
measure comparisons and the independent exact identities already used to prove
their geometry; neither may be weakened to reduce cost.

No individual helper's contribution has been isolated in this increment. The
concurrent thread's unrelated scalar zero-factor failure remains separate work.
