# Declared capability extension

This increment adds exact functionality; it is not a performance-only change.
The existing finite-decimal scalar contract and strict predicate policies remain
in force. No saved-model compatibility adapter or approximate fallback is added.

| Previous outcome | New admitted outcome | Acceptance boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `UnsupportedSurface(Torus)` for native whole/half/quarter tori with representable circular geometry | Exact advanced STEP export, native reimport, validated composition and deterministic replay | `torus_export` and the preserved seam/coplanar/sector fixture workflows |
| Torus family rejection before inspecting irrational boundary geometry | Typed `NonRational` encoding failure with body/entity diagnostics | Original oblique/radical and irrational-band cases, followed by supported export recovery |
| Native R=10, r=5, z>=3 band contact/replay failure | Exact native band, four midpoint split/reload cycles, exact measures, STEP exchange and further recut/clear | Full geometric regression and minimized serialized scalar phase |
| Unavailable scalar proof for selected atan/asin affine identities | Exact principal-angle certificate on the declared rational/quadratic domain | Right-triangle, quadratic-tangent, signed-quadrant, tiny perturbation and serialized replay regressions |
| No ring-torus STEP reader support | Exact latitude/meridian admission with both angular units and source maps | Independent supplied U/V scales/offsets, both masters, reversed traversal, seams, malformed controls and recovery |
| Two undecided full-size rational-sector diagnostics | Native cone/torus sectors with independently checked measures, point classifications, topology and further composition | [Explicit two-slot review](frontier-migration.json) and [public native probe](frontier-extension-summary.json); the unscaled torus volume equality remains undecided, with its identical equality certified after exact division by positive pi |

Positive horn/spindle surfaces remain unsupported rather than invalid ring tori.
Nonpositive radii remain invalid geometry. General torus trims, additional
periodic subdivision, arbitrary analytic Booleans, cone encoding and unrepresentable
numbers remain explicit frontiers. Correct native geometry with an encoding
failure is not counted as a completed STEP workflow.

Native archive expression bytes can in principle change when an unavailable
scalar proof becomes exact or an affine parameter gets its exact rational normal
form. The original matched benchmark receipts and retained archive/STEP artifacts
are compared explicitly. Their final result belongs in `benchmark-summary.json`
and `qualification-summary.json`; the declared capability expansion does not
automatically authorize an unrelated artifact or semantic regression.

The old unsupported torus assertions are replaced by complete exact workflows;
the full original geometry, cutter sizes, near-coincidence controls, cone failures
and same-context recovery remain. Historical logs and source overlays retain
the old behavior and every failed repair. Counts of successful tests or new API
entries are not claims of general kernel completeness or shipped release support.
## Canonical archive expressions in preserved workflows

The completed benchmark campaign detects five changed archive receipts in the
longitudinal torus band, sector similarity matrix and three oblique workflows.
This is separately admitted by the [explicit representation review](approved-receipt-migrations.json).
The [full comparison](receipt-archive-equality.json) covers 173 archive pairs:
113 are identical and 60 change only 238 exactly equal affine pcurve
start-coordinate scalars. Every equality is direct and strict; all non-scalar
fields agree. Both versions of each changed archive revalidate and serialize
identically on the candidate. The original independent workflow assertions
remain and all reconstructed receipts match all three timed trials.

The archive schema is unchanged, but canonical expression bytes intentionally
change in this declared subset. The original no-migration gate remains failed;
162 other benchmark receipts and all 54 separately retained STEP/prism artifacts
are unchanged. No performance-only byte-preservation claim is made for this
feature increment, and no future receipt change is admitted by this review.
