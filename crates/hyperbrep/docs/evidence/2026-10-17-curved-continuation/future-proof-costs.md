# Next proof-cost experiment

This note proposes an investigation, not a retained optimization or an
established explanation of measured latency. The completed main campaign
preserves every receipt but has eight ratios above 1.10. Five are the closed,
partial and composed circle-document workflows; the largest is about 1.261.
The complete rows and raw samples remain in `benchmark-summary.json` and
`benchmark-v1`. Previous cost gates remain open.

First measure where the new and preceding exact normal-form attempts run in
these same completed workflows. Use the existing dispatch/profiling authority,
separating direct rational facts, Laurent/ring/quotient proofs, refinement and
the later congruence attempt. Record failed attempts as well as successes.
Compare construction and reuse with the original cache states, and keep
serialization, whole-image admission and exact output materialization inside
their existing timing boundaries. A microbenchmark alone cannot attribute the
whole-workflow regression.

One hypothesis is that the new final attempt repeats work on expressions
without two eligible sine or cosine occurrences. A bounded eligibility check
or information reused from an earlier attempt might avoid that work. Such a
check may reject only a path for which absence is proved under the actual
recognizer's traversal rules. Nested functions, shared subexpressions,
arithmetic/quotient structure, inverse domains and exhausted scans need
controls. If the scan cannot establish absence, it must preserve the existing
proof opportunity. A loose structural fingerprint is not an equality proof.

Another hypothesis is repeated recovery of already certified exact values.
Inspect the owning scalar facts before adding storage: an existing exact-zero
fact may already be sufficient for some rational projections. Any cache needs
an explicit key, positive certificate, byte/count budget, cancellation/retry
behavior and lifecycle. Do not cache a failed normalization as a permanent
negative result, or infer nonzero rational values from sign alone.

Do not assume a declined attempt has no observable effects. This increment's
first rounding prototype failed because the established comparison schedule
exposes domain facts needed later. Preserve cold and warm mixed-query sequences,
the actual rejected archive, both union orders, two further composition cycles,
nearby unequal controls and deliberately cancelled/exhausted attempts.

Freeze this completed feature revision before editing. Requalify all 172 main
cases and the full scalar/predicate/rounding subset with unchanged assertions,
inputs, iteration counts and receipts. Keep every adverse row, allocation
change, binary size and process resource. Retain an optimization only after
its full correctness and same-completed-contract cost evidence supports it;
do not discharge historical gates with a favorable later sample.
