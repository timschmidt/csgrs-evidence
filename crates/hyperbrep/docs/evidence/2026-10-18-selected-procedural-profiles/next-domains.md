# Remaining development domains

All 28 work packages remain open/in progress. This increment does not complete
the general CAD release or the admission contract for every representation.

1. WP02/WP05b/WP13: extend retained selected surface/contact/branch graphs through
   common-source comparison, whole pcurve images, topology edits and bounded
   replay. Arbitrary recursive projective fields, transformed normal frames and
   independently selected axial translation parameters remain typed frontiers.
   Extend standalone planar-face and extrusion/revolution patch construction
   beyond their coefficient-only paths; selected region extrusion does not
   establish those separate public workflows.
2. WP06/WP07/WP08: replace whole-solid recognizer dependence with general curved
   chart arrangement, embedding, material classification and stitching proofs.
   The current extrusion/slab route is a finite domain, not general curved
   intersection, coincidence or Boolean closure.
3. WP09–WP12: general sweeps/revolutions/lofts, exact offset/shell/fillet construction
   and certified metrics remain dependent on those topology and intersection
   proofs. A valid procedural solid need not have a closed-form exact volume.
4. WP13/WP14: retain semantic authoring provenance, introduce bounded iterative
   replay and explicit coefficient/certificate envelopes, then extend native
   STEP interchange where the exact format contract permits. Selected native
   geometry remains distinct from decimal STEP encoding and explicit fitting.
5. WP21–WP23: installed-package consumers, coherent MSRV/features, production
   bindings, coverage/formal gates and runtime directed fuzz/ASan/lexicase
   campaigns remain open. Source-path native/WASM tests and compile-only fuzz
   checks do not establish those targets.
6. Resource ownership: instrument complete operation work, retained heap,
   coefficient growth and cancellation separately from existing root-refinement
   and promotion counters. Preserve typed exhaustion and recovery. External
   timeouts contain this qualification without claiming an internal bound.
7. Performance follow-up: the preserved partial-height profile contact workflow
   measured about 118 ms before this increment and 369 ms afterward, with
   allocation calls increasing from about 384,276 to 1,277,408. Retain the raw
   samples and exact gap/touch/overlap, volume, replay and classification checks.
   Attribute the cost between the upstream integration and the BRep changes
   before choosing an owning-layer correction. The present extension is not
   an optimization, and the cost obligation remains open.
   The new repeated-slab workflow also requires follow-up: its complete checked
   run takes about 23 seconds, with about 194 million allocation calls and
   24.7 GB of cumulative requested allocation bytes. Those requests are not
   simultaneous resident memory. Preserve the full workload while investigating
   repeated proof construction and allocation churn; the current measurements
   do not identify a cause or establish an internal work/memory bound.

Future changes should start from the qualified source revision, reproduce a
full consumer failure, and retain the current complete workflows, selected
fields, certainty and explicit failure vocabulary. Continue merging upstream
at bounded work checkpoints; do not repair unrelated failures already owned by
the concurrent thread.
