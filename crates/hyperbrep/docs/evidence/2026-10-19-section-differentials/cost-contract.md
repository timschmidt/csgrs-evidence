bytes; report peak child RSS and binary/WASM sizes separately. These are not
live-heap or foreign allocator-internal totals. No build runs during costs.

A >10% median latency change is a review marker, not a significance test.

without evidence. Previous admission costs and the plane/tensor replication
remain valid historical observations; a new run does not erase them. No speedup
claim is intended for this exact-completeness extension. Any deterministic
artifact difference requires an explained migration; no archive schema change
is intended here.
